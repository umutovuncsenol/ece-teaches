How lessons work

Four simple steps from "hello" to your first real conversation.

  1. 1

    Say hello

    Send Ece an email or a WhatsApp message. Tell her where you're from and why you want to learn Turkish.

  2. 2

    Take a free trial lesson

    Meet Ece for 30 minutes, talk about your goals, check your level and experience how the lessons feel.

  3. 3

    Get your plan

    Your goals, lesson days, times and materials are planned around you, not copied from a template.

  4. 4

    Start speaking

    Join an individual or group lesson on Google Meet, at days and times planned around you.

  • Everyday conversation

  • Children and young learners

  • Turkish language and literature

    Explore the language in greater depth through grammar, texts and literature.

Questions you might have

Can't find your answer? Just email Ece.

Do I need to know any Turkish?

Not at all. Lessons start at your level, including zero.

How do online lessons work?

Individual and group lessons take place on Google Meet. Lesson days and times are planned personally with each learner.

Is the trial lesson free?

Yes. The trial lesson is free and lasts 30 minutes. You'll meet Ece, talk about your goals, check your level and try a little Turkish together.

I live in another country. Can we still find a time?

Yes. Tell Ece your time zone and availability, and the lesson schedule will be planned with you.

Can Ece explain in English?

Yes. Lessons focus on Turkish, with B2-level English support when an explanation needs it.

Do you teach children and groups?

Yes. Ece teaches children and adults in both individual and group lessons.

How much do lessons cost?

It depends on the lesson type and personalised programme. Email Ece or message her on WhatsApp and she'll send you the details.
